Sell your software. Skip the busywork.

Section titled “Sell your software. Skip the busywork.”

You build the product. AccessBay handles what happens after the sale. A customer pays, and their licence key is created and emailed. They refund, and the licence is taken back. Nobody has to touch it.

You sell from your own site, so there is no marketplace in the middle. You keep your revenue, and you know exactly who your customers are.

Why sellers choose AccessBay

Section titled “Why sellers choose AccessBay”

Your customers stay yours

Orders, emails and customer details live on your own site. No platform stands between you and your buyers.

Licensing that just works

Set how many sites each licence covers. Every sale gets a key, and every refund takes it back.

Keep your WooCommerce store

A paid order creates the licence. A refund or dispute takes it back. Your store stays as it is.

Updates only buyers can get

Only paying customers get your updates. Download links expire, so a shared link stops working.

Licence emails that arrive

Open source, nothing to hide

From paid order to happy customer

Section titled “From paid order to happy customer”
  1. Add your product. Create the plugin, theme or software you sell. Set its prices and add each release.
  2. Sell it. Your customer pays through WooCommerce. Their licence key is created and sent straight away.
  3. Deliver and protect. Their site activates the key and gets updates. They see their licences, sites and downloads in a portal, without emailing you.

Questions before you sell

Section titled “Questions before you sell”
Do I need WooCommerce?

Today, yes. You take orders through WooCommerce. A checkout built into AccessBay is planned. The roadmap tracks it.

Can I sell through resellers or agencies?

Not yet. It is planned as a Pro feature. A reseller or agency would buy a licence pool (a block of licences bought once), hand out licences to its own clients and get wholesale pricing. The roadmap tracks it.

Is the free plugin complete?

Yes. Free covers licences, updates, the customer portal and licence emails. Pro is a planned extra for later features.

Where does my data live?

On your own WordPress site. Your customers, orders and licences stay with you, and no marketplace sits in between.

What happens on a refund?

The licence is taken back. When a WooCommerce order is refunded or disputed, AccessBay reverses the licence it created.

Can I put licensing inside my own plugin?

Yes. The SDK, currently in beta, adds licence checks, a licence screen and updates to your plugin or theme, so buyers never leave it.

Can I connect my own tools?

Yes. You can connect your own tools, and each one can do only what you allow.

What if my email provider goes down?

Licence emails switch to another provider, and AccessBay alerts you. Your buyers still get their keys.

How do I install it?

Follow the installation guide. It also lists what your server needs.
