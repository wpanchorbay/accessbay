Go to AccessBay > Licences to see the licences you have issued. AccessBay issues them automatically when an order is
paid. Until the first one exists the screen says so. Search, filters, saved views and shareable links are described in
[Finding things](/guide/finding-things/).

## Columns

| Column | What it shows |
|---|---|
| Key | The licence key, masked so only its last four characters show. Select it to open the licence. An "Imported" tag marks a key that was imported rather than generated by AccessBay. |
| Status | The licence's status. See below. |
| Sites | How many sites use the licence out of how many it allows, for example "3 of 5", or "3 of unlimited". |
| Expires | The day the licence ends, or "No expiry". |
| Created | The day the licence was created. |

Click **Expires** or **Created** to sort by it.

## Statuses

- **Not started:** the licence's access has not begun yet.
- **Active:** the licence gives access now.
- **Expired:** its access has ended.
- **Suspended:** it is on hold, either because someone put it on hold or because of a dispute.
- **Revoked:** someone withdrew it.
- **Void:** no purchase counts toward it any more, for example because all of them were reversed.

## Filters and search

- **Status:** Not started, Active, Expired, Suspended, Revoked, Void.
- **Key origin:** Generated, Imported.

The search box finds a licence by its id, by the last four characters of its key, or by the whole key pasted in. A
pasted key is looked up and never saved or shown back.
