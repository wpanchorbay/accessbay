---
title: "Hooks"
description: "AccessBay's public actions and filters."
---
AccessBay has **no public hooks yet**.

The plugin does register WordPress actions and filters for its own use, and you may find their names in the code.
They are internal: they can change or disappear in any release without notice, so don't build on them.

A reference of public actions and filters, each with its arguments and the release it arrived in, is coming in a
later release. Until then, the [REST API](/developers/rest-conventions/) is the supported way to integrate.
