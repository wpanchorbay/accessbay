Roadmap

AccessBay runs on WooCommerce today and grows into a full store for software and digital goods. Everything without a Pro tag is free, and the free plugin works fully on its own. Next, Then and Later show the order we plan to build in, without dates.

  1. Latest

    • WooCommerce
    • Licensing
    • Updates
    • Customer portal
    • Email delivery
    • SDK (beta)

  2. Next

    • Renewals
    • Digital goods
    • Setup wizard

  3. Then

    • Standalone shop
    • Coupons
    • SubscriptionsPro

  4. Later

    • OffersPro
    • Automated campaignsPro
    • AffiliatesPro
    • Resellers and agenciesPro
    • EU VAT and invoicesPro

Sell

Section titled “Sell”
  • WooCommerceShippedPaid orders create licences, and refunds and disputes reverse them.
  • Standalone shopThenCheckout, refunds, invoices and tax rates, with or without WooCommerce.
  • CouponsThenDiscount codes built into the checkout.
  • OffersProLaterBuy X get Y, tiered discounts, add-ons and follow-up deals.

Deliver

Section titled “Deliver”
  • LicensingShippedKeys, activations and seats for every sale.
  • UpdatesShippedOne-click updates in wp-admin, through download links that expire.
  • Customer portalShippedBuyers see their licences, sites and downloads without asking you.
  • Email deliveryShippedLicence emails still arrive if one mail provider fails.
  • SDKBetaLicensing and updates inside your own plugin or theme.
  • Digital goodsNextProtected files with download limits, and refunds that stop access.

Renew

Section titled “Renew”
  • RenewalsNextExpiry reminders and a renewal link that extends the same licence.
  • SubscriptionsProThenRecurring billing with retries, upgrades and seat changes.

Grow

Section titled “Grow”
  • Automated campaignsProLaterEmail sequences that run on their own, including abandoned carts.
  • AffiliatesProLaterReferral links, commissions on renewals and payouts.
  • Resellers and agenciesProLaterSell licence pools to resellers and agencies, who issue licences to their own clients.

Run

Section titled “Run”
  • AdminShippedProducts, orders, licences and health in one place, with search and saved views.
  • REST API and WP-CLIShippedConnect your own tools with keys limited to what they need.
  • Setup wizardNextA guided first run that gets a new store ready to sell.
  • EU VAT and invoicesProLaterVAT tools, PDF invoices and credit notes.
  • Webhooks and reportsProLaterOutbound webhooks, recurring revenue and cohort reports.
  • White-labelProLaterYour branding in place of ours.

Also on our list: more payment gateways, connectors for other stores and licence transfer between customers. Plans change as we learn, and the changelog shows what has actually been released.
